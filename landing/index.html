Privacy

What stays on your machine, stays on your machine.

AIOS is local-first by design. Imports are explicit and user-initiated — never silent.

  • No auto-reading of browser profile directories.
  • No silent scraping of cookies.
  • Provider secrets stay server-side and are never returned raw to the frontend.
  • Audit events store only masked fingerprints — provider, timestamp, action, last4.
